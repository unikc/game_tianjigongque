# Illustration production

Illustrations are keyed by stable IDs and carry scene IDs, cast, location, time, mood, palette, focal point, spoiler level, alt text, generation provenance and crop-safe areas. They never encode choice logic. A scene requests an ID and the renderer selects the best format and crop.

Chibi art uses the shared proportion, line, palette and expression constraints in `chibi/types.ts`. Reserve it for tutorials, achievements, inventory and loading; do not use it to trivialize grief, coercion or punishment.
