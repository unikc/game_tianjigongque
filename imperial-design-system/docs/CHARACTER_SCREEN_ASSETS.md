# Character screen asset briefs

These are production opportunities, not decoration requirements. The current screen uses existing stage portraits and CSS materials until reviewed assets are available.

## P0 — protagonist half-body masters

- Four rank stages, transparent WebP, 900×1400.
- Identical face, hairline, shoulder line and camera across stages.
- Three-quarter body, facing slightly right; top 8% and right 30% remain safe.
- Soft upper-left window light; low-saturation jade and warm ivory.

## P0 — seamless silk-paper grain

- 1024×1024 WebP, extremely low contrast and no visible repeated stain.
- Fiber only: no border, ornament, writing or seal.
- Designed for 2–4% effective opacity.

## P1 — zodiac emblems

- Twelve single-color marks at 96×96 with readable silhouettes at 24px.
- Abstract historical motifs in jade/old gold; no cartoon animals.

## P1 — rank seals

- Eight transparent 160×160 WebP assets from 答应 through 皇后.
- Cinnabar with lightly imperfect ink edges; no fragile microtype.

## P1 — growth ink track

- One reusable 600×48 transparent WebP or CSS mask.
- Dry at one end, concentrated at the other; supports horizontal reveal.

## P1 — relationship portrait masters

- 720×900 WebP, matched bust crop, eye-line, head scale, light direction and background blur.
- Deceased treatment is applied in CSS; do not generate duplicate grey portraits.

## P2 — character-screen ink-wash background

- 1290×900 WebP with a very faint lattice, screen or branch at the edge.
- Center and right side remain safe for identity text; no figures, words or seals.

## P3 — foreground gauze

- 600×1200 transparent WebP, low contrast and confined to an edge.
- Must never cross the protagonist's eyes and should move no more than 2–4px.
