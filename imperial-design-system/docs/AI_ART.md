# AI art workflow

Prompts record character ID, canonical visual anchors, costume, expression, pose, framing, lighting, background treatment, positive prompt, negative prompt, model/version, seed and source rights. Do not prompt with living artists' names or unlicensed character likenesses.

Generate transparent or easily masked portrait layers with safe crops for compact and tall phones. Human review checks identity drift, hands, ornaments, rank accuracy, anachronisms, cultural coherence and accessibility contrast. Approved files use stable asset IDs; swapping providers or revisions must not change scene data.
