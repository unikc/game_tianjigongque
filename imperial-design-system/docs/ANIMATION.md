# Animation

Use named primitives: fade for hierarchy change, slide for spatial continuity, seal-stamp for irreversible authority, portrait-enter for speaker arrival, scene-transition for location/time change, and number-change for stat feedback. Motion must communicate cause and effect. Never delay a choice or hide essential text behind animation.

Durations come from tokens. Under `prefers-reduced-motion`, transitions collapse to 1ms and static state changes remain understandable. Avoid camera shake, repeated bounce and parallax behind dialogue.

## Motion language

- Scene dissolve: 420–550ms opacity transition.
- Portrait arrival: 360ms fade with no more than 8px translation.
- Dialogue sheet: 280ms upward settle.
- Choice press: 120ms tactile compression, followed by a restrained ink wash.
- Relationship change: 450ms gauge draw; direction must also be written in text.
- Decree/rank reveal: 650–900ms unroll followed by one seal stamp.

Only one ambient layer should visibly move on a screen. Idle portrait breathing is limited to 1–1.5% scale over 6–8 seconds and stops under Reduce Motion.
