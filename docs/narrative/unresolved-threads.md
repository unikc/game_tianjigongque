# Unresolved threads

Track promises, secrets, alliances, insults, debts, romantic milestones, political favors, mysteries, important objects and family relationships that remain unresolved.

## Runtime threads carried forward

- Emperor wounded vs. player wounded: changes who owes care, who holds public authority, and which chapter 8 testimony appears.
- Witness saved vs. order-bearer captured: neither is a strictly superior evidence path.
- Gao alive/dead and staff network exposed: affects the palace's access to records and future retaliation risk.
- Emperor favor vs. trust: high favor without trust should feel volatile; high trust without favor should feel politically powerful but emotionally restrained.
- Promotion held: the current title remains valid until imperial bond, public merit, or palace network reaches the next threshold. A later chapter can retry the same promotion.
