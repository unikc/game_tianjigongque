# Timeline

Record dated and chapter-relative events, including off-screen events known only to specific characters.

## Chapters 7–9 consequence chain

| Chapter  | Public event              | Persistent consequence                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| -------- | ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 7 · 春猎 | 御前刺杀                  | The player can shield the emperor, save the witness, or capture the order-bearer. Affection and trust move separately; either the player or emperor may be wounded.                                          |
| 8 · 凤印 | 凤印归属 is contested     | The wounded-emperor route unlocks a public medical testimony; the safe-emperor route unlocks private testimony. Shen and Gu remember whether the player demanded the ledger, shared guilt, or exposed names. |
| 9 · 宫火 | Fire destroys the archive | Gao may live or die. Three incompatible canary records test the review, courier and recipient chains. The returned action reveals a process link, not a villain; false accountability remains playable.      |

Promotion is no longer granted merely for reaching a numbered chapter after chapter 3. From chapter 5 onward, one of three routes—imperial bond, public merit, or palace network—must meet its milestone.

## 崔太后的公开认知与立场转折

| 章节 | 她能合法知道的事                                                 | 立场变化                                                         |
| ---- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 5    | 惠嫔活退、温疏雨正式留档，或赈灾名单转入公案，取决于玩家公开落款 | 明白责任不必只靠处死一人承担；开始允许具体追责                   |
| 6    | 满朝认捐、皇后先开仓，以及是否把问罪与赈运分开                   | 区分崔氏门下的具体责任与对整个家族的替罪式清算                   |
| 10   | 可疑口谕的传递来源、三种公开核验结果，以及玩家已有的摄政依据     | 有依据则具名承认；无依据则质询并给出共署、联署、限期代行三条退路 |
| 11   | 限期摄政是否遵守公开期限                                         | 若玩家守约，她把同一期限贴在宫门内外，为交接作证                 |

她不能从随机种子的幕后真相、私人账路、未公开证词或玩家属性中获得知识。
